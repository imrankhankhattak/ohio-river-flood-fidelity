# Ohio River Flood Fidelity Study - Site Card

## Location
- **City:** Louisville, KY
- **Gauge:** USGS 03294500
- **Coordinates:** 38.2500°N, -85.7500°W (approximate center)

## Study Area
- **Bounding Box:** [-85.8, 38.2, -85.6, 38.3] (≈10×10 km)
- **Area Size:** ~100 km²

## River Characteristics
- **River:** Ohio River
- **Channel Width:** ~600 m (average)
- **Slope:** Low gradient (~0.0001 m/m)

## Flood Event (May 2018)
- **Return Period:** Near 100-year event
- **Peak Discharge:** ~42,000 m³/s at Louisville gauge
- **Peak Stage:** ~18.5 m above flood stage
- **Impact:** Widespread flooding in Louisville metro area

## Data Sources
- **Terrain:**
  - USGS 3DEP LiDAR (10 m resolution, NAVD88)
  - Copernicus DEM GLO-30 (30 m, EGM2008)
  - ALOS AW3D30 DSM (30 m)
- **Hydrology:**
  - USGS gauge 03294500 (discharge & stage)
- **Remote Sensing:**
  - Sentinel-1 (pre-flood: May 1, flood: May 15, 2018)
  - Sentinel-2 (same dates)
- **Land Cover:**
  - ESA WorldCover 10m
- **River Network:**
  - NHD+ for Ohio River
